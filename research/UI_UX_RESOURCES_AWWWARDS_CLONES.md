## Strategic Objective
> I basically just don't trust the first run yet. The internet is the key to me beating a senior frontend engineer/designer. We must weaponize the internet to our favor; I want to beat everyone by a large margin—300%. ## 1. Apple-Style Product Showcase Clones

These are reconstructions of Apple's premium flows (e.g., iPhone 15 Pro) using Three.js, GSAP, and advanced 3D modeling. They serve as textbooks for high-end interaction design.

- **sanidhyy/apple-clone** – Modern iPhone 15 inspired website using Three.js + GSAP animations. [^1]
- **Josekariz/apple-website-clone** – Full React + Tailwind clone with 3D model rendering. [^4]
- **HashimHB/AppleWebsite-Clone** – HTML/CSS/JS focus on Locomotive Scroll and SwiperJS. [^5]
- **Starklord17/Three.js-GSAP** – Practice project for high-end animation structure. [^6]
- **saulkurosaki/APPLE-WEBSITE-CLONE** – Hardware-accelerated 3D performance showcase. [^7]

## 4. Methodology: Harvesting "Internet Juice"

To push beyond generic kits and internalize award-level craft:

1.  **Systematic Mining**: Track topics like `react-three-fiber`, `3d-portfolio`, and `scroll-animation`. [^3][^15]
2.  **Fidelity over Genericness**: Prioritize "clones" as they often contain more micro-detail than generic kits. [^1]
3.  **Pattern Extraction**: Harvest specific "weapons"—killer hero sections, scroll-linked storytelling, or 3D scene wrappers—and integrate them into your own architectural base.
