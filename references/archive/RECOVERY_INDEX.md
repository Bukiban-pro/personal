This index preserves the recovered material as cold memory. Cold memory does not slow boot. It exists so future agents can mine the story, patterns, prompts, and examples before deleting or promoting anything.

## Tiers

| Tier | Meaning | Action |
|------|---------|--------|
| Living doctrine seed | Unique OS/prompt/workflow idea that can directly improve BELT, prep scripts, scenario cards, or agent contracts. | Mine first; promote distilled patterns when proven. |
| Reusable artifact | A prompt, profile, workflow, template, or checklist that can be copied or adapted for a mission. | Keep unless superseded with an explicit replacement. |
| Historical/stale reference | Time-sensitive research, model/tool lists, or broad inspiration. | Keep as source material; verify facts before use. |
| Deletion candidate | Duplicate, secret-bearing, harmful, or no unique value after comparison. | Delete only with ledger entry naming the replacement or risk. |

## Operating Rule

Archive review is a three-pass act:

1. Identify the unique value: prompt, pattern, example, evidence, or story.
2. Decide where it belongs: hot path, reusable artifact, historical reference, or deletion candidate.
3. If deleting, write the replacement or risk in `SCRATCHPAD.md` or `HANDS_LOG.md` first.

If pass 3 cannot name what replaces the file, keep it.
